Q1 Mole Concept

Problem: Find the number of oxygen atoms in 4g of $O_2$.

Ans: Moles of $O_2 = 4/32 = 0.125$ mol. 1 mol $O_2 = 2 \times 6.022 \times 10^{23}$ O atoms. So, $0.125 \times 2 \times 6.022 \times 10^{23} = 1.5055 \times 10^{23}$ O atoms.
