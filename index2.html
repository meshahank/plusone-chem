1-Score Questions (Multiple Choice, One-Word & Assertion-Reasoning)

Q1. Boyle's Law Assertion-Reasoning
PYQ 2024 1 Score

Question: Assertion (A): A gas having a volume of $44\text{ L}$ at $1\text{ atm}$ pressure will compress to $11\text{ L}$ at $4\text{ atm}$ pressure.
Reason (R): According to Boyle’s Law, $P_1V_1 = P_2V_2$.

Answer: Both (A) and (R) are true, and (R) is the correct explanation of (A).
Explanation: $1\text{ atm} \times 44\text{ L} = 4\text{ atm} \times 11\text{ L} = 44\text{ L}\cdot\text{atm}$.
Q2. Isoelectronic Radius Order
PYQ 2023 1 Score

Question: Identify the species among $N^{3-}, O^{2-}, F^{-}, Na^{+}, Mg^{2+}$ that has the largest ionic radius.

Answer: $N^{3-}$ (Nitride ion).
Explanation: All are isoelectronic (10 electrons). Lower nuclear charge ($Z=7$) exerts less inward pull, resulting in the largest size.
Q3. Conjugate Acid-Base Pair
PYQ 2022 1 Score

Question: Write the conjugate acid of $NH_3$ and conjugate base of $H_2O$.

Answer:
• Conjugate acid of $NH_3$ = $NH_4^+$ (add $H^+$)
• Conjugate base of $H_2O$ = $OH^-$ (remove $H^+$)

3-Score Questions (Derivations, VSEPR & Dipole Moments)

Q7. VSEPR Theory & Water Molecule Shape
PYQ 2024 3 Scores

Question: Explain the structure and bond angle of the $H_2O$ molecule on the basis of VSEPR theory.

Answer:
• In $H_2O$, oxygen undergoes $sp^3$ hybridization with a tetrahedral electron geometry ($109.5^\circ$).
• It possesses 2 bond pairs and 2 lone pairs.
• Due to strong lone pair–lone pair and lone pair–bond pair repulsions, the H-O-H bond angle compresses to $104.5^\circ$, resulting in a bent / V-shaped molecular geometry.
Q8. Dipole Moment: $NH_3$ vs $NF_3$
PYQ 2022 3 Scores

Question: Between $NH_3$ and $NF_3$, which has a higher dipole moment? Give reason.

Answer: $NH_3$ has a higher dipole moment.
Reason: In $NH_3$, N is more electronegative than H, so bond dipoles and the lone pair dipole point in the same upward direction. In $NF_3$, fluorine pulls electrons away, opposing the lone pair dipole and canceling out net polarity.
Q9. Relationship between $K_p$ and $K_c$
PYQ 2021 3 Scores

Question: Write the relation between $K_p$ and $K_c$ for the ammonia synthesis reaction: `N₂ (g) + 3H₂ (g) ⇌ 2NH₃ (g)`

Solution:
Formula: $K_p = K_c(RT)^{\Delta n_g}$
• Gaseous moles of products = $2$
• Gaseous moles of reactants = $3 + 1 = 4$
• $\Delta n_g = 2 - 4 = -2$
Result: $K_p = K_c(RT)^{-2}$.

Q10. Molar Volume, Mass & Avogadro Number Numericals
PYQ 2024 4 Scores

Question: Calculate the mass and total number of molecules present in $112\text{ L}$ of carbon dioxide ($CO_2$) gas at STP.

Step-by-Step Solution:
1. Find moles ($n$):
    $n = \frac{\text{Given Volume}}{\text{Molar Volume}} = \frac{112\text{ L}}{22.4\text{ L/mol}} = 5\text{ moles}$.
2. Calculate Mass of $CO_2$:
    $\text{Mass} = n \times \text{Molar Mass} = 5\text{ mol} \times 44\text{ g/mol} = 220\text{ g}$.
3. Calculate Number of Molecules:
    $\text{Molecules} = n \times N_A = 5 \times 6.022 \times 10^{23} = 3.011 \times 10^{24}$ molecules.
